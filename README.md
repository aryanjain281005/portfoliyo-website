# Aryan Jain — portfolio

A night walk through a live WebGL temple, built on Meng To's three studies:

- **Kage**: the world, the camera that travels with the scroll, the foreground cut-outs, the editorial type. The chapters carry my work instead of a temple guide.
- **Towers**: a five-storey pagoda in the same valley that builds itself course by course as you scroll through *The Build*. A clipping plane rises through it and the scaffolding waits above the line. Each course is a layer of my stack.
- **Sketchbook**: the *field notes*. Click any project and a sketchbook opens over the night. You can drag or throw the pages, or drag the brass magnifier across the screenshots.

## What else is in it

- **Things to handle at the temple.**
  - **手配書, the wanted poster (About):** an Edo-style notice with your portrait printed in sepia ink, hung on the same cloth simulation as the cards. Brush it with the cursor; click it to open the author's page.
  - **Plate 00 · the author:** the first spread of the field notes, with your photo taped in and annotated.
  - **絵馬, the ema wall (Path):** each internship or club is a hinoki plaque on a rail. They swing as the cursor brushes past, and turning one over shows the story written on the back.
  - **祭, the festival (Path):** a string of paper lanterns over a ticket to Technovate, the night Raftaar headlined. Pull the stub along its perforation (or tap it) and the back of the ticket shows the sponsorship desk.
  - **名刺, the business card (Contact):** it tilts toward the pointer and turns over. *Take my card* downloads a vCard.
  - **御神籤, the fortune box (footer):** shake it, a numbered stick rises, and one of ten fortunes from your story unfolds.
  - The assets are generated by `tools/make_objects.py` and `tools/make_spreads.py`; the behaviour lives in `js/objects.js`.
- **Music.** The gate offers *Enter with sound*. The track is trimmed, brought down to −17 LUFS with a softened top end, and loops by crossfading its ending into its opening. It downloads quietly at low priority while the preloader runs (choosing silence cancels it), so *Enter with sound* starts at once. If the file is not ready yet, it plays as a stream and the decoded copy takes over at the first loop. It ducks and goes muffled while the field notes are open, and the nav has a toggle (`js/sound.js`).
- **Performance.** Shaders and textures are warmed during the preloader. The quality governor ignores one-off hitches and can climb back up. The pagoda is baked to a handful of draw calls, field-note spreads load on demand, offscreen CSS loops pause, film grain lives in the WebGL composite, and Lenis smooths the wheel. Phones start at 1.5 device pixels and climb to 2 if they keep up, draw at most 60 frames a second, and skip the six lantern point lights. Every shader variant is built by one unculled draw during the preloader, so nothing compiles mid-scroll. `?perf` shows a live frame-time readout.
- **Phones in dark mode.** The page opts out of Android Chrome's Auto Dark Theme (`color-scheme: only light`), which would otherwise repaint the ink on every paper object near-white.

## Run it

No build step. Serve the folder with any static server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4180 --bind 127.0.0.1
```

Then open <http://127.0.0.1:4180/>.

Useful URL flags:
- `?notes=0`–`8` opens the field notes on a project.
- `?shot=N` jumps to a chapter for screenshots.
- `?nogl=1` shows the no-WebGL fallback.

## Layout

```text
index.html                 the whole site: chapters, styles, the three.js world, the pagoda
js/fieldnotes.js           the sketchbook engine (page curl, loupe, riffle) as a dialog
fieldnotes/*.webp          one spread per project, generated from the project screenshots
assets/                    portrait and résumé
secret-pathways-assets/    Kage's three.js r149, fonts (re-subset), plates and cut-outs
```

## Editing content

- Chapter text lives in `index.html`. Each section is commented, for example `chapter I`.
- The stack shown in *The Build* is the `<ol id="stages">` list. The captions over the tower are in `STAGES` inside the pagoda block.
- Projects in the field notes are listed in `PAGES` at the top of `js/fieldnotes.js`. Their spreads are images. `tools/make_spreads.py` regenerates them from text and screenshots. It expects a Sketchbook spread PNG, a `shots/` folder and the fonts listed in its header, all next to it (`pip install pillow numpy fonttools brotli`).
